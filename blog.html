Chlorine & Antioxidant Support

August 18, 2026

Swimming in a pool this summer? So am I — but chlorine comes with a tradeoff I like to be mindful of. Chlorine exposure has been linked to some oxidative stress in the body, and there's a theory that it can put extra demand on glutathione, one of the body's key antioxidants, as it works to neutralize the chemical load.

Before a swim, I like to take a few supportive steps. Some people believe iodine may help the body handle chlorine exposure more easily, though the research here is still pretty limited, so I'd only recommend it after talking to a doctor — especially if you have any thyroid history. I also make sure to get in some vitamin C beforehand, since it's an antioxidant that may help offset some of that oxidative load.

Afterward, I always rinse off right away and moisturize, since chlorine can be drying and irritating to skin.

As always — this is just what works for me, not medical advice. If you're curious about supplementing with iodine or anything else, it's worth running it by your doctor first, especially with any existing thyroid concerns.
