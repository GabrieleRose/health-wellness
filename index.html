Health & Wellness

My health and wellness practices, built from years of research and experience

Hi, my name is Gabriele and I'd like to share some of my health and wellness practices with you today. I have spent years researching and testing different modalities and practices on myself to find out what is most impactful on health.

Practice 01

Circadian Hygiene

Circadian hygiene is a must. We evolved with the sun, and the sun signals to our body to start specific actions based on the time of day. In our modern world we are bombarded by blue light from devices. When viewed at nighttime, these blue lights send daytime signals to our body. This creates a cascade of health issues, including metabolic issues.

Some things I do to mitigate the negative effects of blue light include:

  • Keeping lights low at night
  • Wearing blue-blocking glasses after sunset
  • Putting a red screen filter on all my devices (found in accessibility settings on Apple products)
  • Waking up with the sun
  • Cooking breakfast and eating it outside at sunrise
  • Viewing UV rays about an hour after sunrise
  • Viewing sunset

All of these practices help keep light signaling in line with the sun, so our body can do its job more effectively.

Practice 02

Movement

Movement is one of my favorite health practices. It keeps the heart healthy, keeps the lymphatic system flowing, releases endorphins and keeps energy moving.

Some of my favorite movement practices include:

  • Strength training
  • Somatics
  • Yoga
  • Mobility training
  • Walking
  • Biking

I get in 3–5 days of strength training weekly, as well as yoga and somatics at least 3 times weekly. The rest of the practices vary depending on what's going on in my life. Things are always shifting. It isn't about perfection — balance is key.

Practice 03

Whole Foods Diet

Ultra-processed foods have wreaked havoc on our health. I try to eat mostly whole foods. Again, perfection isn't attainable — nor is it healthy. So I try to live an 80/20 lifestyle: 80% whole foods, 20% minimally processed foods. I don't worry too much about what I'm eating when I go out to a restaurant to enjoy a meal with friends and loved ones.

And there you have it — my core practices for health. I hope you found some helpful tips in here. Come back for more.
